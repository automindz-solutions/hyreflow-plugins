# Recruiter playbooks — intake + durable craft home

This directory is the **curation target** for external recruitment-workflow research and search craft:
Boolean/X-ray guides, sourcing techniques, and end-to-end recruiter-behaviour material (Reddit threads,
posts, podcasts, internal wikis) that we fold into Hyreflow's canonical layer.

## What lives here
- **`boolean-search.md`** — the Boolean / X-ray search craft guide (query construction, operators,
  X-ray patterns, mapping a string onto `people_search`). Authored from well-known patterns now; the
  primary target to merge a contributed Boolean guide into.
- Future distilled playbooks (one durable topic per file).

## The curation pass (raw corpus → canonical)
A contributor may hand us a folder of mixed research. Don't drop it in verbatim. Instead:
1. **Triage** — separate *durable craft* (operators, decision rules, signals) from *anecdote/opinion*.
2. **Distill** — rewrite the durable parts in Hyreflow's voice; one topic per file here.
3. **Promote** — guidance that governs *how the agent talks or decides* folds up into
   [`../../recruiter-craft.md`](../../recruiter-craft.md); sourcing tactics fold into
   [`../../finding-companies-and-contacts.md`](../../finding-companies-and-contacts.md). Keep the search
   *craft* (Boolean/X-ray) here as its durable home.
4. **Link, don't duplicate** (no-loss rule) — when guidance moves up, leave a pointer, not a copy.

## Rules
- **No internal vendor/infra names** in anything here — these files load into the user-facing agent.
  Recruiter craft and search technique only.
- Keep files short and decision-oriented: what to do, when, and why — not exhaustive prose.
